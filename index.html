About the Academy

A Home for the Quran, Built on Trust & Tarbiyah

Tajweedi Quran Academy was founded with one purpose — to make correct, beautiful Quran recitation accessible to every child and adult, wherever they are. Every class blends traditional Tajweed discipline with a warm, patient teaching style suited to young learners.

Our Mission

To teach every student to recite the Quran with correct Tajweed, a beautiful voice, and genuine love for the words of Allah.

Our Vision

A generation of confident Huffaz and Qurra who carry the Quran's light into their daily lives, families, and communities.

Why Parents Trust Us

Experienced, background-checked teachers, flexible scheduling, transparent progress updates, and a genuinely child-friendly classroom manner.

Our Courses

Structured Learning, Step by Step

From first letters to full memorisation — every course is paced to the student and rooted in correct Tajweed.

Nazra Quran

Learn to read the Quran fluently with correct pronunciation, starting from Noorani Qaida through to complete Quran reading.

Beginner Friendly Online & Physical

Hifz Quran

Complete Quran memorisation with a proven daily revision system (Sabaq, Sabqi, Manzil) to make Hifz lasting and strong.

Structured Revision All Ages

Tajweed Rules

Master the rules of Tajweed — Noon Sakinah, Meem Sakinah, Madd, Qalqalah and more — with clear explanation and practice.

Rule-by-Rule Practice Focused

Makharij ul Huroof

Precise training on the correct articulation points of Arabic letters — the foundation of truly beautiful recitation.

Voice Training One-on-One
